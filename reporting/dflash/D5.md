# D5: Anchor density and D-PACE α

- **Tier:** 3
- **Status:** done (2026-11-10)
- **Plan entry:** `dflash_port/EXPERIMENT_MATRIX.md` (D5; caveat in round 7)
- **Evaluation protocol:** [README](README.md#evaluation-protocol)

## What is tested

Do the DFlash training-signal settings matter for the generator?

## Methodology

Generator only, each arm the M0 recipe with one setting changed (`training_recipes/`):
- D-PACE α (per-position loss weighting): 0.5 ★ vs 0.25 and 1.0.
- Anchor density: 512 ★ vs 256 and 1024 anchors per 8192 tokens (0.0625 vs 0.03125 and 0.125).

**Caveats.**
- **The density arms are unpaired.** The validation anchor count uses the same `anchor_density` (`DFlashFamily.max_anchors`), so they are validated on a smaller or larger anchor sample than every other run. Their validation numbers are not strictly comparable with M0's. The α arms are paired with M0 (identical validation anchors).
- **256 anchors ran with the CUDA FlexAttention anchor floor** (8b954dd: at least 8 anchors per microbatch, Q_LEN ≥ 128). Without it the run crashed at update ≈ 20 (RUNLOG 2026-11-10 00:06). The floor raises the anchor count of microbatches shorter than ≈ 248 tokens (≈ 3% of store rows) in training and validation. The first attempt was stopped at update 0, and the rerun resumed from its update-0 checkpoint.

## Measurements

Acceptance per category and per position; offline M4 / M7 / D13 / D14.

Findings (4K pool, one seed):

- **α 0.25 ≈ M0.** Same final score (0.5220), lower and later peak (0.5262 at 500), slightly higher τ (7.177 vs 7.129). Its best offline coefficient gives 0.5291 (M0 0.5289).
- **α 1 is clearly worse**: final 0.5131 / τ 6.898, the lowest of all generator arms, lower at every position.
- **Anchor density:** 256 anchors ends at 0.5226 (≈ M0) and 1024 at 0.5172 (lower, and the slowest run at 66 min). Both are unpaired, see caveats.
- α and density move the row-specificity of the ΔW strongly (dyn_ratio 0.14–0.45) without a matching move in acceptance. As in every arm, the correct donor is not better than a fixed ΔW at s = 2.
- Conclusion for the configuration: keep α 0.5 and 512 anchors.
