- **Base ≡ update 0** in both arms, to every digit (ΔW = 0 at init).
- **Resume:** the generator was cut by the container restart after its update-100 validation; resumed from the update-50 checkpoint, its update-100 validation equals the first attempt's exactly (0.5265, τ 7.187).
- **Offline reproduction:** static bit-exact; generator 0.52647 vs 0.52648 (GPU kernels; reported, not enforced).
- **Training loss** (`train/drafter_loss`): finite throughout; **not clearly decreasing** over 100 updates (mean of updates 1–10: 0.447 / 0.445; 91–100: 0.477 / 0.463, generator / static). The 100-update warmup covers the whole smoke and each update's loss depends on the rows drawn. Not a pass criterion we can claim yet; Tier 1 (1,500 updates) will show it.
- **M4 coefficients** (generator, correct donor, score): 0.5×: 0.5273 · 1×: 0.5282 · 2× (training scale): 0.5265 · 4×: 0.5149 · 8×: 0.4402. Static: 0.5261 · 0.5274 · 0.5284 · 0.5237 · 0.4657.
- **M7 donors** (generator, 2×): correct 0.5265, cross-category 0.5265, fixed 0.5267: no conditioning effect at 100 updates.
- **K=4 continuity:** generator 0.5178, static 0.5189 at 2×, both about 0.009–0.010 below K=1. The static LoRA is the same for every row, so the gap comes from the validation anchor draw over packed rows, not from the generator. **K=4 numbers compare only with K=4 numbers.** For the generator, K=4 is also outside its trained K (recorded as `beyond_trained_examples`).
- **D13** (200 generated LoRAs): dyn_ratio 0.0005, cos_in 0.9999, cos_cross 0.9995, centroid accuracy 0.875, η² 0.76, effective rank 1.69. The LoRAs are nearly identical at 100 updates; small category structure exists.
- **D14:** 2,197,010,912 generator parameters; 20 adapted modules, rank 32; factors 8.5 MB per LoRA; 3.52 ms per generator call; 4.57 ms for 8 batched cohorts; 0.66 ms to merge ΔW (H100, bf16).
- **Cost and memory (sizes Tier 1):** about 3.1 s per update (generator) and 2.2 s (static) including data; process RSS up to 114 GiB (activation RAM cache, cap 120 GiB); generator checkpoint 13.2 GB (with optimizer), static 25.8 MB. Two trainings in parallel fit the 512 GB container.
