## What is tested

How does scaling the generated (and static) ΔW at inference change acceptance? Is there evidence that the best coefficient varies per row, which would point towards a dynamic coefficient?

## Methodology

Offline sweep s ∈ {0.5, 1, 2 ★, 4, 8} (coefficient = b·c/scale). No training. Same rows, cohorts and anchors for every s. EAGLE-3 GEN-008 swept s in serving only.

## Measurements

Mean per category for each s. Per-row table: each evaluation row's acceptance for every s, its best s, and the headroom of a per-row oracle over the best fixed s.
