## What is tested

Can every M0 run read target activations from a shared offline store instead of a live vLLM extractor, without changing any number?

## Methodology

Fill the 4K store (layers [2,10,18,26,34]+36, bf16) with `fill_store.sh` under `--strict-provenance`. Then re-extract 32 rows directly through vLLM and compare with the store (`comparison` in `<store>/fills/<time>.json`).

## Measurements

Store identity (extractor, vLLM build, target hash), entry count, size, SHA256, and `bit_identical` / `max_abs_diff`.
