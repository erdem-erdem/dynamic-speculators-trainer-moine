## What is tested

Can every M0 run read target activations from a shared offline store instead of a live vLLM extractor, without changing any number?

## Methodology

Fill the 4K store (layers [2,10,18,26,34]+36, bf16) with `fill_store.sh` under `--strict-provenance`. Then re-extract 32 rows directly through vLLM and compare with the store (`comparison` in `<store>/fills/<time>.json`).

## Measurements

Store identity (extractor, vLLM build, target hash), entry count, size, SHA256, and `bit_identical` / `max_abs_diff`.

## Results

- **Drafter:** `z-lab/Qwen3-8B-DFlash-b16@9b41424b`, converted and validated (58 weights). `preparation.json` sha256 2e35068a…; aux layers [2,10,18,26,34], block 16, mask id 151669, 5 draft layers.
- **Store identity:** Qwen/Qwen3-8B@b968826d, layers [2,10,18,26,34,36], bf16. Extractor: vLLM 0.31.0 wheel (dist-info RECORD sha256 20ca263c…), target weights sha256 8f511322….
- **4K fill:** 5,200 rows (4 × 1,000 train + 4 × 100 validation of `*-00000`; 4 × 100 validation of `*-00001` test and `*-00002` donors). 10,400 entries (summary + full), 8.90M tokens, 437.5 GB extracted, 333 GB on disk, 670 s.
- **OOD v2:** 150 rows, 300 entries, 127,552 tokens, 6.27 GB extracted, 92 s.
- **Online ≡ offline (32 rows):** full hidden states and token ids **bit-identical** (max |diff| 0.0). Prompt summaries max |diff| **4.0**: one bf16 step at the largest values (|x| up to 604; spacing 4 above 512), batch-dependent rounding in vLLM. Every run reads the same stored summaries, so training and evaluation are self-consistent; an online run would not reproduce a stored summary bit for bit.
