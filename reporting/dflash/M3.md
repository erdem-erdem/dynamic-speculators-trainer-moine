## What is tested

Does generator capacity matter? Decoder width first; other axes only if width matters.

## Methodology

`architecture.decoder_width` 256 / 512 ★ (= M0) / 1024, with everything else as in M0 (attention targets, rank 32, per-module heads, 4K pool, 1,500 updates, seed 42, 200 fixed validation rows).

**Where the parameters are.** Each output head is `Linear(decoder_width → LoRA factor elements)`, so generator parameters ≈ (decoder_width + 1) × LoRA elements (4.26M here), plus a few million in the encoder and trunk. Width therefore scales the generator almost linearly: 1.11B / 2.20B / 4.38B.

## Measurements

Acceptance per category and per position; D13; D14 cost.

Findings (4K pool, one seed):

- **Width does not matter in the measured range.** Decoder 256 (half the parameters) matches M0: final 0.5223 vs 0.5220, best offline coefficient 0.5296 vs 0.5289. Decoder 1024 (twice the parameters) ends lower (0.5193), consistent with more overfitting.
- So, per the plan, encoder_hidden / residual_blocks / condition_dim are not run.
- Together with D3 (per-type head sharing, 0.45B, no loss), this means the generator can be made much smaller without a measured cost. Per-type sharing with decoder 256 would be ≈ 0.22B (not run).
