## What is tested

What does a generated LoRA cost at inference: generator parameters, latency per call, factor size and the cost of merging ΔW into the drafter? (Round 3: accept-only wins are "stronger, not faster" until P5 measures tok/s.)

## Methodology

Microbenchmark in the offline eval (`offline_eval.generator_cost`) on the same H100, bf16, 20 timed repeats after a warm-up: one generator call (one cohort), 8 cohorts batched (`forward_cohorts`), and W + s·B·A for every adapted module.
