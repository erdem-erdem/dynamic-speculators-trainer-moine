## What is tested

Does adapting the fusion of the 5 target layers help?

## Methodology

DFlash projects the concatenated hidden states of the five target layers with `fc` before the draft layers read them. `adapter.fc: true` adds a generated LoRA on `fc` (its own layer slot after the last adapted layer) on top of the M0 attention adapters. Off ★ = M0. Everything else is as in M0 (rank 32, 4K pool, 1,500 updates, seed 42, 200 fixed validation rows). Generator only.

## Measurements

Acceptance per category and per position; offline M4 / M7 / D13 / D14.

Findings (4K pool, one seed):

- **The fc adapter does not help.** Peak 0.5286 vs 0.5289, final 0.5210 vs 0.5220, best offline coefficient 0.5285 vs 0.5289. It adds 0.40B generator parameters (2.60B total), one more adapted module (21) and +43% merge time (0.97 vs 0.68 ms).

- **Correct donor < fixed ΔW at s = 2** (−0.0023), as in every arm so far.
- Per-position curves overlap within 0.004.
