- **Finding + fix: DFlash microbatch padding.** Upstream `select_anchors` never anchors in the last `block_size` packed positions. Upstream packs to a fixed 8192, so t
         +hat tail is normally padding, but generator microbatches pack to the sum of row lengths. Without a fix, the tail of the last row in each microbatch could never be an anc
         +hor, and a pack of ≤ `block_size` tokens crashed with `IndexError` (found by the tiny DFlash campaign run). `DFlashFamily.microbatch` now right-pads every packed tensor 
         +by `block_size` (loss mask 0, document −1, as `CollateFn`). The anchor count still uses the unpadded length. `check_drafter` records `block_size` and logs the geometry (
         +block, mask, sample_from_anchor, attention, anchor density, validation seed, kv path, loss); `microbatch` raises if `check_drafter` has not run.
